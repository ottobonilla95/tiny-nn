Each hidden neuron compares your pixels with one geometric pattern. Max pooling selects the strongest match in each shape family. A low score or a close tie produces “Unknown.” Try moving a shape, adding gaps, or making a circle a little squarer.

This first model handles isolated geometric shapes. Freehand drawings are an additional experiment; synthetic test accuracy does not guarantee freehand accuracy. View the held-out test report
